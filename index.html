About

A family daycare, on purpose.

Care happens in a real home in Willoughby, Township of Langley. Mornings are unhurried. Play is the work of the day. Parents know who is looking after their child — the same familiar faces, every day.

Home, not a hallway

One household, one rhythm. Children settle faster when the room already feels like a home.

Known by name

A small group means we can follow a child’s pace — extra cuddles, extra time at the puzzle, extra outdoor air.

Mixed ages, gently

Infants and preschoolers share the day the way siblings do — younger ones watch, older ones help, everyone belongs.

Small group

Six children. That’s the whole train.

We keep the group small on purpose — so play stays calm, meals stay sociable, and no one disappears into a crowd.
